# The inbox

The **{{ui.eingang}}** holds everything that is waiting for you, across all projects. The number on the button and on the projects in the bar shows how much there is.

## What appears there

| Item | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **{{ui.eingang.art.frage}}** | The session needs a decision and names options. | Click an option, with an addition if you like, or write your own answer. |
| **{{ui.eingang.art.sitzung}}** | A session is done or waits for an approval. | **{{ui.eingang.hin}}** |
| **{{ui.eingang.art.hinweis}}** | The session points something out, often with a place in the artifact. | Look, then **{{ui.eingang.gesehen}}** |
| **{{ui.eingang.art.schlange}}** | Tasks wait until the session accepts input. | Wait or **{{ui.eingang.jetzt}}** |
| **{{ui.eingang.art.anfrage}}** | The session has requested photos or audio from the phone. | Deliver on the phone, see [Phone and tablet](06-handy-und-tablet.md) |
| **{{ui.eingang.art.lieferung}}** | Something has arrived from the phone that no session has picked up yet. | Look at it |
| **{{ui.eingang.art.dienst}}** | A service is to be offered to all projects and needs your confirmation. | **{{ui.eingang.pruefen}}** |

## Questions

You answer a question in the inbox; the answer is typed into the session. If you settle the matter in the conversation, the deck clears the question by itself as soon as the session carries on. The session can also withdraw a question. With **{{ui.eingang.abraeumen}}** you take it out of the inbox without answering.

A session that has many questions at once does better with a questionnaire, see [Artifacts](03-artefakte.md).

## Notes in the artifact pane

A note also appears as a band above the view it refers to. **{{ui.band.ansehen}}** jumps to the place.
